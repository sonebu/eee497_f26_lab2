EEE 497 - Lab 02
Estimate a tone's frequency

The Pluto SDR is transmitting something for this session, 
tune the receiver to 433 MHz to see it.

Record the IQ, then process the file.

Ubuntu:

    rtl_sdr -f 433000000 -s 2048000 -g 35 -n 8192000 capture.iq

Windows, from the folder that contains rtl_sdr.exe:

    rtl_sdr.exe -f 433000000 -s 2048000 -g 35 -n 8192000 capture.iq

Two signals are present in the IQ recording:
- An always-on pilot tone. This is one of the signals to measure. It is the
  reference frequency, and it cancels the Pluto frequency drift and the
  RTL-SDR frequency drift.
- 8 short pulses in a row, on the positive side of the pilot. The pattern
  repeats, with a longer silence between each loop. Use GQRX or SDR# to see
  its form. Try different FFT sizes and window selections (rectangular, Hann,
  and others).

Goals:

- Find the pilot frequency.
- Find each pulse in time and in frequency.
- Measure each pulse frequency minus the pilot.

functions_library.py has the pieces this measurement needs. Putting them
together is the main body of the work.

Report, for each of the 8 pulses, in order of appearance:

- the frequency minus the pilot, in Hz
- the standard deviation of that difference over the repeats, in Hz

Deliverables:

- the recording
- the code
- the table showing your measurements 
- a lab report (later, deadline TBA)

The main goal is to get each pulse's frequency measurement stdev under 10 Hz, if possible.
